# Optional public shop

The public shop is disabled on new installations. To enable it:

1. Serve My Stuff at HTTPS.
2. Set `MYSTUFF_PUBLIC_URL` to that exact origin.
3. Open Settings, enable Public shop, review the exposure statement, and confirm it.
4. Configure the shop title, introduction, privacy copy, and default listing currency.

A private item remains absent until it is owned, has at least one selected photo, and its listing is published. The public projection includes the item name, brand, category, colour, size, listing description, asking amount/currency, and selected photos. It excludes owner/profile, notes, private price, purchase details, groups, source URLs, import facts, and unselected photos.

Visitors can open a listing and leave a comment and contact details for the administrator. Messages stay in the private Shop inquiries screen. My Stuff sends no email and processes no payment.

Unpublishing, moving to Wishlist or Trash, or receiving a definitive return immediately removes the listing and its image responses. Shop pages and images use `no-store`; do not override this at a proxy or CDN.
